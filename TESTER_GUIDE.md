# Whisprly — Tester Guide

Thanks for testing Whisprly! Here's everything you need to get started.

---

## What is Whisprly?

Whisprly is a desktop dictation app. Hold a hotkey, speak, release — your words are instantly transcribed and typed into whatever you were working on (Word, Chrome, Slack, anything).

---

## Installation

1. Run the `.msi` installer
2. Launch **Whisprly** from the Start menu
3. Sign in with your Google account

---

## Using the app

### Record and transcribe

1. Click into any text field in any app (e.g. a browser search bar, Word document, chat box)
2. Hold **Ctrl + Win** and speak
3. Release both keys when done
4. Whisprly transcribes your speech and types it automatically

> The floating pill at the bottom of your screen shows the current status — orange = recording, yellow = transcribing.

### Your transcript history

Everything you dictate is saved in the Home tab. You can:
- **Click** any entry to copy it to clipboard
- **Hover** over an entry to reveal the delete (🗑) button
- **Clear all** using the button at the top of the feed

---

## Tips

- Works in **any app** — Word, Chrome, VS Code, Notion, Slack, etc.
- Speak naturally — punctuation like "comma", "period", "new line" may be recognised
- If transcription seems slow, check your internet connection
- Click the floating pill during recording to **cancel**
